  2. 2026 water delivery app runs a water dispenser delivery and service business: clients, filter changes, routes and billing. web development

    one web app for the office and the technicians on the road. it knows which dispenser needs a new filter and when, plans the week's routes and keeps the billing data in one place.

    • dashboard of overdue and upcoming filter changes across every client location
    • weekly route planning on real roads, split between technicians
    • map of all locations, colour-coded by how urgent the next visit is
    • tablet and phone view for technicians: today's stops, one-tap navigation, confirm the visit and the next one is scheduled
    • clients with several locations, invoice data and monthly settlement
    • enquiries from the website tracked from first contact to closed

    how it's used

    1. the office keeps every client, location and dispenser in the app, with the date of the last filter change
    2. each week the app lists what is due and plans a route for every technician
    3. technicians follow the route on their phone and confirm each visit, which schedules the next change
    4. sales log enquiries from the website and phone and turn them into clients
    5. at the end of the month the office settles subscriptions and extra services from the visit history
    status
    in production, maintained
    built with
    next.js, typescript, postgres
    runs on
    the web: desktop, tablet and phone

    admin panel on desktop
